# P017

## Title: Optimizing MEC Migrations for Energy and User Quality of Experience 1

**Supervisor:** Liam Murphy

**Tags:** Mobile/wireless networking, performance evaluation, simulation, iFogSim2

**Theme:** Software Engineering and Distributed Systems

**Stream:** Both

**Description:** Cloud computing has undergone phenomenal growth in recent years, but one of its major disadvantages is the distance between cloud servers and end-users. This problem has led to the development of Multi-Access Edge Computing (MEC) which aims to place cloud resources closer to the end-user. Previous projects explored the trade-off between energy efficiency and a user's Quality of Experience (QoE) by examining different initial placement strategies for application modules in the fog computing simulation toolkit, iFogSim2. This project will continue to develop this approach e.g. by considering other models of QoE and/or energy consumption, and/or other sources of variation in the system.

### Mandatory deliverables:
1. Conduct a comprehensive literature review of QoE and Energy Efficiency in MEC.
2. Propose, refine and specify a simulation or prototyping solution to an issue in this area.
3. Basic implementation and evaluation of the proposed solution in 2.

### Discretionary deliverables:
1. More realistic implementation of the proposed solution.
2. Comprehensive evaluation of the proposed solution.
